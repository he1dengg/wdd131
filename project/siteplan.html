Website Planning Document

Cozy Bean Cafe

1. Site Name

Name: Cozy Bean Cafe

Reason: This name represents a welcoming, warm, and comfortable environment for coffee lovers in the local community. It is simple, memorable, and clearly states what the business is about. Optional domain: cozybeancafe.com.

2. Site Purpose

The site provides a central hub for the local community to explore the cafe's menu, discover seasonal specials, find the physical store location and operating hours, and purchase premium roasted coffee beans online.

3. Scenarios

  • What are your current business hours and where are you located?
  • Do you offer dairy-free or vegan options for your seasonal lattes?

4. Color Schema

The following colors are used to evoke a warm, coffee-inspired atmosphere. They are applied directly to this site plan document.

  • Cream Background: #F4EFE6 - Used for the main body background to keep it easy on the eyes.
  • Dark Brown: #4A3B32 - Used for all headings and primary paragraph text for high contrast and readability.
  • Warm Orange: #D97A3B - Used for accents, borders, and highlighting important elements.

5. Typography

We are using two main Google Fonts for this project, which are also applied to this document:

  • Heading Font: 'Merriweather', serif. Applied to h1, h2, and h3 tags to give a classic, reliable feel.
  • Body Font: 'Open Sans', sans-serif. Applied to all paragraph text, lists, and general information for clean, modern readability.
